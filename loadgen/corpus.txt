# Load-test corpus. Sentence lengths are the single biggest driver of TTS latency, so a
# corpus of uniformly short prompts produces a flattering and meaningless benchmark.
#
# The distribution here is deliberately mixed to approximate assistant speech: mostly
# short-to-medium replies, with a tail of long ones. Lines starting with # are ignored.
